Question 1: NIFTY 50 – Top 10 Constituents by Weight

What to do: To identify the Top 10 constituents of NIFTY 50 by index weight for the current year and the past 10 years, and visualize their relative importance using pie charts.

Steps:
    Official NIFTY 50 constituent data was collected year-wise.
    For each year, the top 10 stocks by index weight (%) were selected.
    Pie charts were created to visualize relative index weight distribution.
    Original index weights were preserved in labels, while visualization normalization was applied only for plotting.

Observations:
    A small number of large-cap stocks dominate the NIFTY 50 index.
    Over time, sectoral leadership shifts are visible (e.g., banking and IT dominance in recent years).
    The index exhibits high concentration, indicating that index performance is strongly influenced by a few heavyweight stocks.

Question 2: Gold vs Crude Oil – Price Correlation Analysis

What to do: To analyze the relationship between Gold and Crude Oil prices over the last 10 years and study their correlation behavior.

Steps:
    Daily closing prices for Gold and Crude Oil were downloaded using yfinance.
    Data was stored in CSV format to ensure reproducibility.
    Prices were min–max normalized to enable comparison on the same scale.
    Both assets were plotted on the same chart.
    Additional year-wise plots were generated to observe changing correlations over time.

Observations:
    Gold and Crude Oil do not maintain a stable correlation across years.
    During periods of economic uncertainty, Gold often behaves as a safe-haven asset, diverging from Crude Oil.
    Year-wise analysis reveals that macroeconomic shocks significantly alter commodity relationships.

Question 3: Sectoral Market Composition Analysis

What to do: To analyze the sectoral composition of the Indian equity market using:
        1. Market Capitalization (relative comparison)
        2. Traded Volume (liquidity comparison)

for the past 10 years.

Sectors Analyzed:
                Auto
                Banking
                Energy
                FMCG
                Pharma


Steps: Two complementary analyses were performed:

1. Market Capitalization (Index-Based Proxy)
    Since exact sector-wise NSE market capitalization data is not directly available in comparable form, sector index levels were used as a proxy for relative market size.
    Sector indices are market-cap weighted, making them suitable for relative comparison.
    Year-end index close values were used for each sector.
    Donut charts were plotted to visualize relative sector dominance.

2. Traded Volume (₹ Crores)
    Daily Turnover (₹ Cr) values were aggregated year-wise.
    This represents total trading activity and liquidity for each sector.
    Donut charts were plotted to show distribution of traded volume across sectors.

Observations:
    Banking and Energy sectors generally dominate in terms of market capitalization proxy.
    FMCG and Pharma show relatively lower volatility, indicating defensive sector characteristics.
    Traded volume analysis highlights periods of heightened investor interest in cyclical sectors such as Auto and Banking.
    Market capitalization and trading volume dominance do not always coincide, highlighting the difference between value concentration and liquidity concentration.
